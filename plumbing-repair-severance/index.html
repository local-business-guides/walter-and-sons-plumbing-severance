Details that help explain the problem

Before calling, take a moment to gather information. You can use this short checklist:

  • Identify the fixture or area involved, and give its location in the home.
  • Describe the symptom in simple terms, such as a visible drip or a change in drainage.
  • Note when you first noticed it and whether anything has changed since.
  • Tell us if the issue is affecting one fixture or more than one, if you know.
